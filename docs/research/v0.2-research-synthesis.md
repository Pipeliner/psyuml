# PsyUML v0.2 — research synthesis (deep research, June 2026)

A five-angle deep-research pass commissioned to **inform a v0.2 *evolution*** of PsyUML, and to **verify, extend, or correct** the attached prior synthesis ([`3cdc7244-deepresearchreport.md`], reproduced under `docs/research/` if archived). Each claim below carries a source and a confidence (High/Med/Low); adversarial caveats are called out. This is a *knowledge-organization* input, not a clinical evidence base — see §7.

> **Through-line (the single most important finding).** The tools PsyUML unifies — genograms, case formulations, parts-models, CAT maps — are **clinically popular but weakly evidenced *as interventions*, and formulations are not reliably reproducible between clinicians.** That argues for **humility as a first-class design stance**: PsyUML is a *shared-meaning / communication* aid, **not** a validated diagnostic or therapeutic instrument, and v0.2 must keep saying so. (Flinn 2015; Eells 2025; Joseph 2023 — High.)

## 1. Collaborative formulation is the right centre of gravity

- BPS/DCP *Good Practice Guidelines on the use of psychological formulation* (2011) define formulation as a **collaboratively-constructed, biopsychosocial hypothesis based on personal meaning — explicitly distinct from psychiatric diagnosis**. (https://explore.bps.org.uk/content/report-guideline/bpsrep.2011.rep100 — High)
- Thrower et al. (2024, PRISMA review of 19 qualitative papers): facilitators of collaborative formulation are **simplicity, thorough preparation, "doing-with" activities (timelines, *diagrams*), client empowerment, and a safe environment**; barriers are **therapist dominance, complexity, and that some clients find it unhelpful or distressing**. (https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/cpp.2998 — High)
- Sharing a formulation is **double-edged**: empowering *and* potentially distressing — distress spikes when it is perceived as **inaccurate, identity-threatening, or imposed** (Evans 2020 — Med; Pain/Chadwick/Abba on diagrammatic CBT-psychosis formulation: it raised understanding but could be saddening, conditional on perceived accuracy — Med).
- **Formulations are not reliably reproducible.** Inter-rater reliability runs **slight (.1–.4) to substantial**, agreement is good on descriptive content but **poor on inferential/explanatory content**, and improves with training and multi-rater averaging (single judge ≈0.37 → five judges ≈0.72). (Flinn et al. 2015 — High)
- **Outcome evidence is thin by the field's own admission**: "relatively little empirical research on case formulation … no consensus on how best to develop one" (Eells 2025, *World Psychiatry* — High). Team-formulation reviews call outcomes "promising" but low-to-medium quality (overclaim flag).
- **Adversarial counterweight (lived experience):** even "good," collaborative formulations can harm; the clinician–client power asymmetry "will always privilege the opinions of the clinician," and guidelines permit team formulations made *without* and *withheld from* the person (Aves — Med, single advocacy source, but a verifiable structural point about provenance/consent).

**Verdict on the attached synthesis ("collaborative, provenance-labelled, revisable, plain-language; imposed/over-complex = barriers"): CONFIRMED, with two qualifications** — (a) "collaborative" and "revisable" are *values*, under-evidenced empirically (formulation stability is barely studied); (b) "plain/simple" must not become *reductive* (over-simplification invalidates). **Do not claim the notation improves outcomes.**

## 2. CAT's Sequential Diagrammatic Reformulation is the strongest native ancestor

- The SDR ("the map") is literally a **node-and-transition graph**: reciprocal roles at the centre, *procedures* drawn as loops emanating from and returning to them. Its design rationale is **memorability/usability** — Ryle wanted "an adequately complex but memorable and useable way of describing patterns" (this directly vindicates PsyUML's premise). (ACAT — High)
- The SDR's organising centre **shifted over time** (negative emotional states → reciprocal roles), so the central abstraction is **not fixed** — a strong argument for ontology-neutrality. (ACAT — High)
- SDR + reformulation letter are **jointly created** with the client (co-creation is definitional). Service users describe it as an "eye-opener … chaos to clarity" that **reduces self-blame**, *and* as "exposing/upsetting/scary" — the map is not neutral. (Taplin 2018; Balmain 2021 — Med/High)
- **The diagram is a vehicle for the alliance, not an independent active ingredient**: a small-N study found reformulation tools produced felt insight but not consistent measured change; change was attributed to the relationship operating *through* the tools (Tyrer & Masterson 2019 — Med). **No controlled evidence isolates the SDR component.** CAT-as-therapy has a moderate but **contested** base (meta-analytic ES≈0.86 from uncontrolled pre–post; Calvert & Kellett: "limited," "largely bypassed the controlled phase"). (High)

**Verdict: HOLD as a first-class ancestor, but SCOPE it.** Lift the **graph machinery** (typed nodes, reciprocal/directed edges, *sequential procedures*, **exits as intervention markers**) and especially the **three named loop topologies** — **trap** (self-confirming loop), **dilemma** (false-binary fork), **snag** (self-truncating loop) — which are **plausibly school-agnostic**. Keep **"reciprocal role"** and **"observing eye"** as *optional, theory-tagged* node types, not the mandatory centre.

## 3. The added modalities reduce to four archetypes (and most are not real notations)

Only **CFT three-circles** and the **Moreno sociogram** are genuinely standardized visual notations; the **ACT Matrix** is the cleanest *sorter*. The **ACT Hexaflex** lines are decorative (no transition semantics); **DBT chain analysis** and **PTMF** are worksheets / guided-question scripts that yield prose; **structural family maps** have a symbol *vocabulary* but no governing standard; the **Attachment Mapping Protocol** is a single 2023 source. (Sources in the matrix below — Med/High.)

The seven reduce to **four diagram archetypes**: **(a) field/sorter** (ACT Matrix 2×2, CFT 3-circle, social atom), **(b) parts** (Hexaflex processes, CFT systems), **(c) typed-edge relationship network** (structural family, sociogram, AMP), **(d) linear path with intervention points** (DBT chain, PTMF story). The **most universal primitives** (≥4 modalities): *self/actor*, *context/situation*, *affect-body node*, *action-tendency with toward/away valence*, and an *intervention/change vector*.

**Verdict: CONFIRMED that diagrams converge on a small set of reusable primitives — but the attached synthesis OVERSTATES the visual maturity of several sources.** v0.2 should add families where the formalism is real (Cycle/Pattern/Field/Parts) and treat PTMF/DBT as **guided-question or linear-path *templates*** rather than claiming them as notations.

## 4. Notation science: design for novices, and test symbols before freezing them

- Moody's **Physics of Notations** (IEEE TSE 2009) gives 9 principles for *cognitively effective* notation; **semantic transparency** (a novice can infer meaning from appearance) is the key lever for a lay audience. (High)
- **Novices/participants design more transparent symbols than experts** — in the i\* redesign, participatory symbols raised spontaneous interpretation ~**300%**, hit-rate ~**4×** (≈17%→67%), and cut errors **>80%**. (Caire 2013; Moody, Heymans & Matulevičius 2010 — Med; single research group, direction solid / magnitude indicative.)
- **Adversarial caveats:** PoN is a *design theory*, **weakly validated in practice** (van der Linden & Hadar 2018 SLR: adopted "with little critical thought," "few verifiable details"), several principles **can't be formalized**, "semantic transparency" is itself a **contested construct**, and **PoN is not accessibility-validated** (colour reliance unproven for colourblind/grayscale). (High) — *this vindicates v0.1's "no meaning by colour alone, monochrome-printable" rule as ahead of PoN.*
- **ISO 9186** provides a *validated* comprehension-test method (open-production + 3 judges) with a **~67% comprehension bar (≈two-thirds) for general symbols, 85% for safety-critical**.

**Verdict on "participatory symbol design + comprehension testing BEFORE freezing the asset library": STRONGLY SUPPORTED.** A runnable protocol (for v0.2's evaluation suite): **(0)** participatory generation of candidate symbols with laypeople; **(1)** ISO-9186 comprehension test, gate **≥67%** general / **≥85%** for risk/self-harm markers, and report the **wrong/opposite-meaning rate** (a confidently-misread symbol is disqualifying); **(2)** matching test for discriminability; **(3)** time-to-correct + 5-second recall + confidence rating; **(4)** dual-coding lift (icon vs icon+label). ≥30 per audience (clients, trainees, clinicians) for the quantitative gate.

## 5. Diagram families + audience profiles — where cross-school translation holds and breaks

The "**families + profiles, not one hard-coded vocabulary**" architecture is **well-supported**. Translation holds where schools share *visual primitives* (loops, typed nodes, typed relational edges, sorters). It **breaks** on theory-laden primitives: CAT **reciprocal roles** don't port to CBT/behavioural/systemic; **PTMF/narrative** are conversational choreography more than static diagrams; **IFS "parts"** must stay ontology-neutral. v0.2's families should be: **Field · Cycle · Parts · Pattern · Journey · Change · Ritual · Composite**, each with **clinician / client / picture (low-literacy)** profiles (the picture profile is well-motivated — Padesky's reduce-to-pictures, child play-formats — though its symbols must themselves pass §4 comprehension testing).

## 6. Ethics, safety, cultural extensibility, and FHIR interop

- **Ontology-neutral "parts" is well-motivated:** the IFS evidence base is small relative to adoption, its "evidence-based" branding leans on a **stale (2018-archived) SAMHSA/NREPP listing** (marketing flag), and reification may be **harmful where reality-testing is fragile** (Society for the Advancement of Psychotherapy critique — Med, one critical camp, not consensus; 2025 scoping review calls IFS "promising," pilot-level — Med).
- **Cultural-permission flag is warranted:** cultural genograms are a *training/awareness* tool (Hardy & Laszloffy — High); closed/sacred symbols (e.g., white-sage smudging) are not free iconography (appropriation — principle sound, best citation is non-academic; re-anchor). Spiritual-resource mapping is established (spiritual genograms — Med).
- **FHIR has no native "formulation" or "genogram" resource.** Recommended **lossy export** mapping (limits stated): whole diagram → **`Composition`** (sections = zones, with narrative + references); the assessment text → **`ClinicalImpression`** (`summary` + `finding`; note it deliberately *doesn't* fix a diagnosis → reinforces non-diagnostic default); internal nodes / cycles / symptoms → **`Observation`** (use `Condition` only where a node *is* a diagnosable problem); relational/genogram structure → **`FamilyMemberHistory` + `List`** (+ Genetic-Pedigree profile) — **edge semantics (cutoff/fusion/conflict/attachment-quality) do not survive; a custom extension is required and the PsyUML diagram remains the source of truth**; treatment direction → **`CarePlan` + `Goal`**. (hl7.org/fhir — High)
- **Honest evidence base:** genogram *therapeutic effectiveness* "has not been empirically explored or verified" (Joseph 2023 — High); formulation reliability is modest (Flinn 2015 — High).

## Prioritized recommendations for v0.2 (well-evidenced ✔ vs design-inference ◇)

1. ✔ **Keep the humility stance front-and-centre** — non-diagnostic default, "unvalidated, supports-not-replaces," provenance + confidence on every element. (Already in v0.1; v0.2 should *formalize confidence* and surface it.) Reliability/outcome gaps make this load-bearing.
2. ✔ **Adopt the families + audience-profiles architecture** (Field/Cycle/Parts/Pattern/Journey/Change/Ritual/Composite × clinician/client/picture). Maps v0.1's 12 types onto 8 families; adds the picture/low-literacy profile.
3. ✔/◇ **Add a Pattern family from CAT** — the graph machinery + **trap/dilemma/snag loop topologies** + **exits**; reciprocal-role/observing-eye as *optional* node tags. (Design machinery ✔; "improves therapy" ✗ — frame as expressivity.)
4. ✔ **Move comprehension testing earlier and make it a gate** — bake the ISO-9186 + participatory protocol into the evaluation suite (Tier-A automatable harness + Tier-B human studies); test client/picture symbols **before** freezing them.
5. ◇ **Spec a FHIR *export* (lossy, document-shaped)** — Composition + ClinicalImpression + Observation + (FamilyMemberHistory/List) + CarePlan/Goal, with limits documented; the diagram stays source-of-truth. (Implement later; previously shelved.)
6. ✔ **Cultural-permission flag + closed-practice declaration** on spiritual/ritual symbols; **ontology-neutral internal nodes** (part/mode/ego-state/voice/role/state).
7. ◇ **Do NOT overclaim the new modalities as notations** — represent PTMF/DBT as guided-question / linear-path templates; only standardize symbols that pass comprehension testing.

## Limitations of this synthesis
Several primary texts (Moody 2009, the i\* papers, IFS scoping review, some Wiley/Springer/ResearchGate full-texts) were paywalled or unfetchable; affected numeric claims rest on cross-corroborated abstracts/snippets and are flagged Med. The IFS-reification critique and the appropriation citation are single/popular-press sources representing a position, not consensus. **No validated cross-school *visual* formulation notation was found to exist — which is itself the gap v0.2 addresses, as a collaboration language, not an outcome claim.**
