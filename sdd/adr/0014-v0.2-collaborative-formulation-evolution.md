# ADR-0014: PsyUML v0.2 — a collaborative-formulation-first evolution

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-06-15
- **Deciders:** project owner + maintainer
- **Spec / REQ touched:** docs/specification/psyuml-v0.2.0.md (new) / REQ-DIAGRAM-FAMILIES, REQ-AUDIENCE-PROFILES, REQ-NOTATION-TESTING, REQ-INTEROP-FHIR, REQ-COLLAB, REQ-ACCESSIBILITY

## Context
An attached external synthesis plus a fresh five-angle deep research
([`docs/research/v0.2-research-synthesis.md`](../../docs/research/v0.2-research-synthesis.md)) argued that
v0.1 — strong on notation/accessibility — under-named its true centre. The evidence:

- **Collaborative formulation** is the cross-school centre of gravity (BPS 2011; Thrower 2024), but
  formulations are **not reliably reproducible** (Flinn 2015) and the tools PsyUML unifies are
  **weakly evidenced as interventions** (Eells 2025; Joseph 2023 on genograms). → humility must be
  a first-class stance, not a footnote.
- **CAT's SDR** is the strongest native visual ancestor — lift its graph machinery + the
  **trap/dilemma/snag** loop topologies + **exits**, but keep "reciprocal role" *optional*
  (ontology-neutral; the SDR's own centre shifted over time).
- The additional modalities reduce to **four archetypes**; most (PTMF, DBT, Hexaflex) are
  worksheets, not notations — don't overclaim them.
- **Notation science**: design symbols *with* novices and **comprehension-test them (ISO 9186, ≥67%/85%)
  before freezing** the asset library; PoN is a design theory, weakly validated and **not**
  accessibility-validated (which vindicates v0.1's monochrome-redundant rule).
- **FHIR** has no native formulation/genogram resource; only a *lossy, document-shaped* export is
  honest.

The owner chose: **evolution (call it v0.2)**, and **spec + docs + system design + phased roadmap, then
start implementing** the highest-value piece.

## Decision
Adopt **v0.2 as a backward-compatible evolution** of v0.1 (no `.psyuml` document changes required;
all v0.1 types/glyphs/validators/editor unchanged), captured in `docs/specification/psyuml-v0.2.0.md`:

1. **Reframe the centre as collaborative formulation** (spec §1) and **formalize provenance + confidence
   as two orthogonal axes** (§3; adds `contested` provenance). Keep the standing **honesty clause**
   (unvalidated; communication aid; no outcome claims) binding.
2. **Group the 12 diagram types into 8 families** (Field/Cycle/Parts/Pattern/Journey/Change/Ritual/
   Composite) × **3 audience profiles** (clinician/client/picture), §2.
3. **Add CAT-derived Pattern semantics** — trap/dilemma/snag loop topologies + exits-as-intervention,
   with reciprocal-role/observing-eye as *optional* tags; **ontology-neutral internal nodes** (§4).
4. **Make comprehension testing a release gate** (ISO 9186 + participatory design), Tier-A automatable
   + Tier-B human studies (§5).
5. **Spec a lossy FHIR export** (Composition/ClinicalImpression/Observation/FamilyMemberHistory+List/
   CarePlan) with documented limits; diagram stays source-of-truth (§7).
6. **Sharpen ethics** — non-diagnostic default, ontology-neutral parts, cultural-permission flags,
   audience-scoped exports (§6).

**Implemented in this release (M11):** the **families + audience-profiles registry** in
`@psyuml/profiles` (`FAMILIES`, `FAMILY_OF`, `familyOf`, `diagramsInFamily`, `AUDIENCE_PROFILES`,
`audienceProfile`) + tests. The rest is spec + a phased roadmap (M12–M16).

## Consequences
- **Positive:** names the evidence-backed centre; gives users a families map + audience profiles;
  inherits CAT's expressivity without its metaphysics; bakes the "test symbols before freezing"
  discipline in; documents an honest FHIR path. Nothing in v0.1 breaks (MINOR semver).
- **Cost / limits:** most v0.2 surface is spec + roadmap, not yet code (Pattern rendering, picture
  profile, FHIR export, Tier-B studies are M12–M16). The families registry is metadata; the editor
  doesn't yet group by family or switch audience profile. The reframing is a *value* commitment —
  the evidence supports collaboration/humility, **not** that the notation improves outcomes.
- **Impact:** `docs/specification/psyuml-v0.2.0.md`, `docs/research/v0.2-research-synthesis.md` (new);
  `packages/profiles/index.ts` (+ test); traceability REQ-DIAGRAM-FAMILIES / REQ-AUDIENCE-PROFILES
  (in-progress), REQ-NOTATION-TESTING (planned), REQ-INTEROP-FHIR (spec'd); ROADMAP M11–M16.

## Alternatives considered
- **Clean v2.0 rewrite from a new metamodel.** Rejected by the owner (evolution, v0.2) — and the
  research shows v0.1's ontology already covers the convergent primitives; a rewrite would waste the
  working implementation for little gain.
- **Adopt the additional modalities as first-class notations.** Rejected — PTMF/DBT/Hexaflex are
  worksheets/question-maps; representing them as guided-question/linear-path *templates* is honest.
- **Round-trip FHIR as source of truth.** Rejected — FHIR can't carry genogram edge semantics; a
  lossy export with the diagram as source-of-truth is the only faithful option.
