# Impact — `examples/`

**Purpose:** the canonical example corpus — real PsyUML models (`.psyuml` JSON) and their
golden renders (`.svg`), used as regression fixtures and demos. Grows toward the full §H
worked case, one diagram per type as milestones land.
**Status:** active (M1: State Map)
**Spec anchor / REQ:** REQ-STATE-MAP, REQ-CORE-ONTOLOGY, REQ-NOTATION

## Upstream (this depends on)
- `@psyuml/model` (the schema these files must satisfy) and `@psyuml/render` (produces the goldens).
- `../docs/specification/psyuml-v0.1.0.md` §E (diagram types), §H (the worked case).

## Downstream (depends on this) — blast radius
> **Blast radius: medium.** `packages/model` and `packages/render` tests read these as fixtures;
> `apps/web` imports `state-map.svg` for its on-screen render. A model-schema or renderer change
> can require regenerating the goldens here.

## Change checklist
- [ ] Every `.psyuml` must `parseModel` cleanly; every golden `.svg` is produced by the renderer (do not hand-edit).
- [ ] Regenerate goldens when the renderer changes (delete the `.svg`, re-run `pnpm test`).
- [ ] Ran `node sdd/check.mjs` (green).
